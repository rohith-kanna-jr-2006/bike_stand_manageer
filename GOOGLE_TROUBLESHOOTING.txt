GOOGLE LOGIN TROUBLESHOOTING
============================

The error "redirect_uri_mismatch" means your Google Cloud Console settings are slightly wrong.

Please verify these EXACT settings in the Google Cloud Console:

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials
2. Click the pencil icon on your "SecureCycle Local" credential.

CHECKLIST (Must be exact):
--------------------------

[ ] Authorized JavaScript origins
    MUST contain: http://localhost:3000
    (Make sure there is NO trailing slash at the end. "http://localhost:3000/" is WRONG.)

[ ] Authorized redirect URIs
    MUST contain: http://localhost:3000
    MUST contain: http://localhost:3000/auth/google/callback

Important Notes:
- If you just changed these settings, it can take 5-10 minutes to take effect.
- Clear your browser cache or try an Incognito window if it still fails after 10 minutes.

PORT ISSUE
----------
Your terminal showed "Something is already running on port 3002".
This means the backend crashed but didn't close properly.

To fix this:
1. Open Task Manager
2. End any "Node.js" processes.
3. OR run this command in terminal: npx kill-port 3002
